GOOGLE SIGN-IN + WELCOME EMAIL  (set up once)
=============================================

WHAT IT DOES
- A "Sign in" button appears at the top right of every page, and a Welcome popup asks new visitors to sign in with Google.
- The first time someone signs in, the site sends "Welcome to TraderWorkStation" to their Google email address.
- Signed-in people see all their orders under "My orders", the assistant knows what they bought, and checkout uses their Google email.
- Signing in is optional. Without GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID in .env the sign-in buttons simply do not appear.

PART 1 - GOOGLE (about 10 minutes)
1. Open https://console.cloud.google.com and create a project (any name, for example TraderWorkStation).
2. Menu > APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen (may be called "Google Auth Platform"):
   - User type: External. App name: TraderWorkStation. Support email: your email. Developer contact: your email.
   - Scopes: keep only the defaults (email, profile, openid). Nothing extra is needed.
   - When you are ready for the public, press "Publish app" so any Google account can sign in.
     (While it is in "Testing", only the test users you list can sign in.)
3. Menu > APIs & Services > Credentials > Create credentials > OAuth client ID:
   - Application type: Web application. Name: TraderWorkStation website.
   - Authorized JavaScript origins (add each address you use, exactly, with the port):
         http://localhost:3000          (use your real port, for example 5000)
         https://YOUR-DOMAIN            (when the site is live)
     Note: localhost and 127.0.0.1 are different origins. Use the one you type in the browser.
   - You do NOT need any "Authorized redirect URI".
4. Copy the Client ID (it ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com). It is not secret.
5. In the project's .env file add:
         GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=1234567890-abcdef.apps.googleusercontent.com
   Then restart the server. The terminal should say "Google sign-in: on".

PART 2 - SENDING THE WELCOME EMAIL (choose one)
A) Gmail (simple, free, about 500 emails per day)
   1. In your Google account turn on 2-Step Verification.
   2. Google Account > Security > App passwords > create one named "TraderWorkStation". Copy the 16 letters.
   3. In .env add:
         SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
         SMTP_PORT=465
         SMTP_SECURE=1
         SMTP_USER=yourname@gmail.com
         SMTP_PASS=the16letterapppassword
      Optional: MAIL_FROM=TraderWorkStation <yourname@gmail.com>
   Gmail only lets you send "from" your own address, so the email will show that address as the sender.
   For a business address like hello@yourdomain.com use option B or a business email service.
B) Resend (good for a business domain)
   Verify your domain in Resend, then add RESEND_API_KEY=... and MAIL_FROM=TraderWorkStation <hello@yourdomain.com>.
   Resend only sends to other people after the domain is verified.
Run   npm install   once (it installs the email sender), then restart.

PART 3 - TEST
1. Open the site in a private/incognito window. After about 3 seconds the Welcome popup appears. Or press "Sign in".
2. Sign in with a Google account. You should see "Welcome ... We sent a welcome email".
3. Check that inbox (and spam). Also check Admin > Users (welcome "Sent") and Admin > Emails (result and any error).
4. Buy something with that same Google email and see it under My orders.

COMMON PROBLEMS
- Popup says "origin_mismatch" / button missing: the address in the browser is not in "Authorized JavaScript origins". Add it exactly.
- "Access blocked / app not verified": the consent screen is still in Testing. Add yourself as a test user, or publish the app.
- Sign-in works but no email arrives: open Admin > Emails and read the error. For Gmail the usual causes are a wrong app password
  or 2-Step Verification not turned on.
- Browser blocks the prompt: allow third-party sign-in / pop-ups for the site.

PRIVACY
The site receives only the visitor's name, email address and profile picture from Google, never a password.
The Privacy Policy page already says so. A visitor can delete their own account on the My orders page.
