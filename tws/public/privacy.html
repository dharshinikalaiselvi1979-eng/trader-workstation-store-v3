Privacy Policy

Draft. To be reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

We keep your email address, order details and any message you send, so we can deliver your purchase, send receipts, handle refunds and reply to you. Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles your card details under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to create your account, show your orders, personalise the assistant and send you a welcome email. We never get your Google password. If you use the chat assistant, we keep the questions you type (without your name or email) to improve the answers. To ask us to delete your data, use the contact form.
