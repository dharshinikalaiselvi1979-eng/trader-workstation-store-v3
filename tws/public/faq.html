What do I get?

PDF guides for self-directed traders. One guide includes an Excel worksheet. All material is educational.

How do I receive my guide?

As soon as Stripe confirms payment, your order page shows a licence key and a download button, and we email a receipt with the same link.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Request a full refund within 14 days of payment, for any reason, from your order page. After a refund, the download and licence key stop working.

Do you give financial advice or trade for me?

No. This is education only.

Is payment secure?

Payment is handled by Stripe Checkout. Card details never reach this site.

I lost my order link

Use Find my orders. We email a secure link to the address you paid with.

Sometimes. Enter a code in the cart before paying.
